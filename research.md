    <p class="section-kicker">Research</p>
    <h2>From lithospheric deformation to earthquake rupture</h2>
  </div>

  <p>
    From minerals to mantle, my research deciphers the multi-scale physics that drive the Wilson cycle, bridging grain-scale processes, melt/fluid-rock interactions, and mantle convection to understand Earth's dynamic evolution. My research integrates geological fieldwork, laboratory experiments, and theoretical frameworks to develop next-generation, physics-based 2D/3D numerical models that simulate these complex systems.
  </p>
  <p>My work specifically focuses on two key areas:</p>
  <ol>
    <li><strong>Long-term tectonic evolution:</strong> I develop advanced magmatic-thermo-mechanical models that couple grain-size evolution with melt/fluid transport to unravel the initiation and life cycle of major tectonic features, from mid-ocean ridges to subduction zones.</li>
    <li><strong>Short-term earthquake dynamics:</strong> I incorporate laboratory-derived, thermally activated friction laws into seismic cycle models to simulate realistic earthquake rupture dynamics and aftershock sequences along transform faults.</li>
  </ol>
</div>

<div class="section-block">
  <div class="section-heading">
    <p class="section-kicker">Themes</p>
    <h2>Core research directions</h2>
  </div>

  <div class="feature-grid">
    <article class="feature-card">
      <span class="feature-index">Research I</span>
      <h3>Mid-ocean ridges dynamics</h3>
      <p>I investigate how magma supply, grain-size evolution, and brittle–ductile weakening shape faulting, crustal thickness, and tectonic structure at mid-ocean ridges.</p>
    </article>

    <article class="feature-card">
      <span class="feature-index">Research II</span>
      <h3>Subduction zone evolution</h3>
      <p>I investigate how fluids, melts, and rheological feedbacks reduce lithospheric strength, drive thinning and destruction, and shape the long-term evolution of convergent margins.</p>
    </article>

    <article class="feature-card">
      <span class="feature-index">Research III</span>
      <h3>Transform fault seismicity</h3>
      <p>I integrate laboratory-derived friction and constitutive laws into seismic cycle models to explain rupture complexity and transform-fault earthquake dynamics.</p>
    </article>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="section-block">
  <div class="section-heading">
    <p class="section-kicker">Methods</p>
    <h2>How I work</h2>
  </div>

  <div class="stack-list">
    <article class="stack-card">
      <h3>Numerical modeling</h3>
      <p>Physics-based 2D/3D geodynamic and seismic-cycle simulations designed to connect grain-scale mechanisms with plate-scale behavior.</p>
    </article>
    <article class="stack-card">
      <h3>Laboratory constraints</h3>
      <p>Experimentally informed constitutive laws for friction, healing, and rheological weakening that can be embedded in dynamic models.</p>
    </article>
    <article class="stack-card">
      <h3>Comparative systems</h3>
      <p>Cross-system analysis spanning oceanic ridges, subduction zones, transform faults, and planetary settings to identify shared physical controls.</p>
    </article>
  </div>
