    <h2>Biography</h2>
  </div>

  <p>
    Mingqi Liu is a Postdoctoral Researcher at the <a href="https://www.geologie.ens.fr/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Laboratoire de Géologie</a> de l'<a href="https://www.ens.psl.eu/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">École Normale Supérieure - PSL</a> in Paris. His research aims to develop a unified, physics-based framework for lithospheric dynamics across timescales, linking long-term tectonic evolution with short-term earthquake rupture.
  </p>
  <p>
    Using advanced 2D and 3D numerical modeling, constrained by laboratory experiments and geological and geophysical observations, he investigates how microphysical processes—such as grain-size evolution, fluid–melt transport, and thermally activated frictional healing—interact with lithosphere deformation and fault dynamics.
  </p>
  <p>
    His research spans geodynamics, earthquake physics, numerical modeling, and planetary tectonics.
  </p>
</div>
